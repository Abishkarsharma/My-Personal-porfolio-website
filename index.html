About me

the short version

I went to school in Baglung and came to Kathmandu for work and study. I got into programming by building small websites and working through Harvard's free CS50 course, and I've kept building things ever since.

At work I collect and clean data, find what's interesting in it, and turn it into reports people can actually use. At college I'm learning the engineering side properly: how to design software that holds up, not just code that runs.

I like projects where both of those meet: taking messy information and building something simple that makes sense of it.
